Project 16 • Scientific Evaluation & Research Paper Mapping

Machine Learning & Deep Learning Prediction of Drug Synergy in Breast Cancer

Direct validation against Frontiers in Pharmacology (2025) by M. Mozaffarilegha & S. Gharaghani

Problem Statement & Research Objective

Traditional computational approaches predominantly rely on chemical structures or conventional drug signatures, which overlook cellular resistance mechanisms. This platform implements Drug Resistance Signatures (DRS)—derived from transcriptomic variations between resistant and sensitive cancer cell lines—to accurately predict synergistic drug combinations for breast cancer (specifically ER+ MCF-7 and T47D).

Evaluated ML & DL Algorithms

  • • Linear Regression: Baseline linear synergy benchmark.
  • • LASSO (L1 Regularization): Sparse feature selection.
  • • Random Forest (RF): Ensemble decision trees.
  • • AdaBoost: Sequential adaptive boosting.
  • • XGBoost: Regularized gradient boosted trees.
  • • SynergyX (Deep Learning): Multi-layer neural network.

Five Evaluated Cancer Datasets

  • • DrugComb: High-throughput multi-dose combinations.
  • • DrugCombDB: Curated clinical & pre-clinical pairs.
  • • O'Neil: 23,000+ experimental cancer combination screens.
  • • OncologyScreen (PRISM): Cell line viability assays.
  • • NCI-ALMANAC: Over 3 million FDA-approved drug pairs.

Biological Validation on Breast Cancer Cell Lines

The paper specifically validated that combining aromatase inhibitors with antimetabolites/targeted therapies overcomes acquired endocrine resistance in ER+ breast cancer:

Anastrozole + MTX MCF7 Score: +12.59
Anastrozole + MTX T47D Score: +23.87
Letrozole + MTX MCF7 Score: +11.20
Imatinib + 5-FU (Control) Antagonistic: -4.20
